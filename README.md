# Calm Oasis

Build a responsive, highly calming digital wellness and mind-relaxation web app designed for everyday individuals, workers, and students who want to destress, find focus, or unwind.



Visual Styling & Theme:

- Color Palette: Use a serene palette with soft Sage Green (#E8F0EC), Muted Ocean Blue (#E6F0FA), Warm Cream (#FAF8F5), and subtle Lavender (#F2EFE9) accents.

- Card Style: Soft, rounded floating cards (border-radius: 20px) with subtle glassmorphism blur and soft drop shadows. No harsh dark borders.

- Typography: Rounded, gentle sans-serif font with generous line height and ample whitespace so the interface feels airy, uncluttered, and peaceful.



Interface Layout & Main Pages:



1. Top Welcome Banner:

   - A soothing greeting: "Welcome to your digital sanctuary. Take a deep breath."

   - A daily peaceful affirmation quote card that refreshes softly.



2. Quick Mood Selector:

   - Four rounded, gentle mood buttons: "Feeling Overwhelmed", "Need Deep Focus", "Anxious / Restless", "Ready to Unwind".

   - Tapping a button automatically customizes the active audio track and chat suggestions.



3. Interactive Breathing & Relaxation Visualizer:

   - A soft, glowing circular element in the center of the screen that smoothly expands (Inhale) and contracts (Exhale) on a gentle 4-7-8 rhythm to guide physical relaxation.



4. Ambient Sound & Focus Player:

   - Clean audio cards for: "Rain on Window", "Lofi Focus Beats", "Forest Birds & Stream", and "438Hz Healing Waves".

   - Include a smooth play/pause button, volume slider, and subtle wave animation when audio is active.



5. AI Companion Chat Interface:

   - A minimal, distraction-free chat box titled "Your AI Mind Coach".

   - Use the built-in AI connector with an empathetic system prompt: "You are a warm, supportive, and calming mind coach. Keep answers short, empathetic, and reassuring to help the user destress."

   - Display pre-made prompt suggestion pills below the chat: "Help me calm down right now", "I'm feeling distracted at work", "Guide me through a 2-minute reset".



Mobile Optimization:

- Ensure all touch targets and buttons are large, rounded, and easy to tap on mobile screens.

- Add smooth fade-in transitions between sections to create a floating, gentle experience.
