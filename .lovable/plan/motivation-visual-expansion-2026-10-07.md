# Motivation Visual Expansion

## Goal
Make the Motivation-only sanctuary feel immediately uplifting for someone arriving with low energy, using realistic sunrise and mountain-path imagery, encouraging quotes, and purposeful motion. Calm, Focus, Sleep, Confidence, account pages, and signed-out visitors remain unchanged.

## What will change
- Replace the current abstract Motivation welcome background with a premium, realistic sunrise-over-mountain-path scene in indigo shadows, teal atmosphere, and sunrise gold.
- Keep the existing Velora serif styling and welcome message, with a stronger image veil so every word remains readable on mobile and desktop.
- Add restrained visual motion: slowly changing morning light, subtle atmospheric drift, and a gentle forward-path effect. The existing pause control will stop all Motivation motion; reduced-motion settings will show a polished still scene.
- Expand the daily Motivation quote experience with a curated set of short, supportive lines for low-energy moments. Quotes will encourage one achievable next step without pressure, shame, medical claims, or aggressive “hustle” language.
- Add a slim visual momentum strip below the quote using three symbolic milestones—Begin, Move, Become—presented as scenery-led visual cues rather than statistics or gamification.
- Carry the Motivation palette into the home-page cards through selective indigo, teal, aqua, amber, and gold accents, while preserving Velora’s warm cream surfaces and airy layout.

## Experience rules
- The enhanced theme appears only when the saved profile goal is exactly `Motivation`.
- No new profile questions, permissions, database fields, or AI calls.
- Quotes remain supportive wellness content, not therapy, diagnosis, or guaranteed outcomes.
- The generated mountain image will be stored as a local app asset, not loaded from an external image host.

## Verification
- Test switching from another goal to Motivation and back, confirming the theme appears and disappears correctly.
- Check the welcome, quotes, and milestone visuals at mobile and desktop sizes for readability, overflow, and clean spacing.
- Verify motion pause and reduced-motion behavior.
- Confirm other goals and account pages retain their existing appearance, with no browser or build errors.
